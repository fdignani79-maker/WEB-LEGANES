Useful information

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official trial to sign for the club?

No. These are paid football training and development programs; they do not guarantee a signing or contract.

What do the 1-week and 15-day programs include?

Residence accommodation, three meals a day, football training suited to the player's level, official Real Murcia CF training apparel and Elite Fútbol Scouting apparel, a professional photo session, first-team match tickets subject to schedule and availability, access to sports facilities (gym and physiotherapy), a stadium tour, ongoing support, and an arrival transfer from Alicante Airport, Murcia Airport or Murcia train station.

What do the 1- to 10-month programs include?

They include all standard services, including Elite Fútbol Scouting apparel, ongoing support and access to sports facilities (gym and physiotherapy). Nutrition services are also included. The 3- and 10-month programs additionally include coaching sessions.

Is there an extra charge for arrival transfers?

No. All programs include arrival transfer to the residence from Alicante Airport, Murcia Airport or Murcia train station.

Do optional services cost extra?

Yes. Optional services not expressly included in the selected program are quoted separately.

How can I receive a technical and tactical evaluation?

The club provides the player's technical and tactical evaluation when requested by Elite Fútbol Scouting.

Which programs include coaching sessions?

The 3-month and full-season (10-month) programs include coaching sessions.

Can players take part in official matches?

Participation depends on availability, playing level, registration requirements and federation places. It is not guaranteed.

How do I book a place?

A deposit of 30% of the program price is required. Payment terms and the balance schedule are provided in writing before booking.
